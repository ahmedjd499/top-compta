## Brand & Style
This design system defines an authoritative, prestigious, and technologically advanced experience tailored for corporate leaders, SMEs, freelancers, and real estate partnerships (SCI). It bridges classic French fiduciary rigor with modern digital accounting automation (electronic invoicing, secure cloud document management / GED, and direct transactional settlement).

The aesthetic marries **Corporate / Modern** precision with **Refined Glassmorphism** and subtle **Tonal Layering**:
- **Trust & Statut:** Conveyed through deep midnight and royal blue tones, giving the assurance of institutional-grade security, tax compliance, and long-term business advisory.
- **Prestige & Value:** Warm gilded amber and champagne bronze accents lift the interface above conventional utilitarian software, signaling bespoke executive attention.
- **Clarity & Serenity:** Airy slate surfaces, crisp architectural lines, and micro-deliberate typographic contrast remove the cognitive friction and anxiety typically linked to accounting and tax deadlines.

## Colors & Palette Nuancée
Le système chromatique est directement extrait de l'emblème historique du logo **TOP-COMPTA** (le Jaune doré solaire et le Bleu royal d'autorité), décliné en une double gamme harmonieuse de 4 nuances complémentaires, optimisées pour le contraste et la hiérarchie visuelle.

### 1. La Famille des Bleus (Confiance, Rigueur Fiducière & Modernité)
- **Bleu Corporate (`#22437f`) :** La teinte exacte du logo. Utilisée pour les ancrages de marque, la typographie d'autorité, les boutons d'action majeurs et les badges institutionnels.
- **Bleu Pétrole (`#0f3b5c`) :** Nuance profonde, fiducière et statutaire. Idéale pour les conteneurs premium, headers nocturnes, footers et contrastes exécutifs.
- **Bleu Clair (`#60a5fa`, soft `#dbeafe`, givré `#eff6ff`) :** Nuance aérée et lumineuse. Utilisée pour les fonds de cartes, les survols, les badges secondaires et la respiration des interfaces.
- **Bleu Turquoise (`#0891b2` / `#06b6d4`) :** Nuance technologique vive. Symbolise la GED cloud, la facturation électronique, l'automatisation et les flux bancaires directs.

### 2. La Famille des Jaunes (Énergie, Valeur & Statut)
- **Jaune Vif (`#e7b821` / `#f59e0b`) :** L'éclat doré solaire du logo "TOP". Utilisé pour capter le regard, les conteneurs de conversion hero, les boutons de paiement direct et les points focaux.
- **Jaune Moutarde (`#b45309` / `#d97706`) :** Nuance ambrée chaleureuse à haut contraste (5.02:1 sur blanc). Réservée aux textes d'alerte statutaires, aux étiquettes "Recommandé" et aux bordures d'accentuation.
- **Jaune Citron (`#facc15` / `#fef08a`) :** Nuance fraîche et lumineuse. Utilisée pour les étoiles de notation Trustpilot, les étincelles d'innovation et les micro-indicateurs d'état.
- **Jaune Clair (`#fef9c3` / `#fffbeb`) :** Nuance crème ivoire délicate. Idéale pour les fonds de surbrillance doux, les bannières d'information sans agresser l'œil et les cartes réconfortantes.

### 3. Surfaces & Neutres
- **Backgrounds & Canvases (`#ffffff`, `#f8f9ff`, `#e5eeff`) :** Surfaces nettes à subtil reflet bleuté maintenant une lisibilité totale et un grand confort de lecture.
- **Contraste Typographique (`#0b1c30` / `#45464d`) :** Lisibilité maximale pour l'ensemble des chiffres et mentions légales.

## Typography
The pairing combines **Space Grotesk** for primary and section headers with **Plus Jakarta Sans** for running body copy, interface forms, and data labels. 

- **Space Grotesk** delivers a contemporary, structured, and confident aesthetic reminiscent of progressive European fintech and audit leaders. It anchors numerical pricing, metric highlights, and core commercial promises.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** brings ergonomic softness, warm humanist proportions, and immaculate legibility on screen, ensuring financial small print, fiscal terms, and multi-step inquiry forms remain effortless to navigate.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a centered 12-column responsive fluid grid capped at a maximum width of `1280px` for optimal reading comfort.

- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12 columns, `1.5rem` (24px) gutters, and a minimum outer margin of `3rem` (48px). Pricing structures sit comfortably across a 4-card matrix or a featured 3-card balance.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, `1.25rem` (20px) gutters, and `2rem` (32px) outer page margins. Grids collapse into 2x2 formations.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `1rem` (16px) gutters, with `1rem` (16px) horizontal outer canvas safe areas. Component cards stack vertically with pricing cards adopting full-width swipeable or layered views.

Sectional rhythm alternates intentionally between dense, information-rich modules (FAQ, step-by-step processes) buffered by generous vertical padding (`5rem` to `7rem` on desktop, `3rem` on mobile) to eliminate cognitive clutter.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth in this system avoids heavy, muddy drop shadows in favor of luminous ambient glows, crisp 1-pixel borders, and soft glass-frosted planes:

- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** Pure `#FFFFFF` or tinted neutral `#F8FAFC`. Devoid of shadows; delineated purely by hairline `#E2E8F0` dividers.
- **Level 1 (Subtle Lift - Cards & Grids):** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)`, shadow: `0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Featured Focus & Active Plan):** For highlighted cards (e.g., *Formule Confort*), uses a refined gradient border with a primary-tinted glow: shadow `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(30, 64, 175, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(30, 64, 175, 0.04)`, enclosed with a subtle top border highlight of `rgba(30, 64, 175, 0.6)`.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Nav & Modals):** Sticky header and floating action docks utilize glassmorphism: `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`, background: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)` (or `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.9)` on dark executive banners), bounded by a `1px` border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)` or `rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.7)`.

## Shapes
A roundedness level of `2` provides a polished, welcoming, yet strictly corporate appearance:
- Standard elements (input fields, buttons, interactive badges): `0.5rem` (8px).
- Containers & Cards (pricing modules, testimonial blocks, timeline steps): `1rem` (16px).
- Executive Hero Blocks & Featured Modals: `1.5rem` (24px).
- Status Pills, Notification Chips, and User Avatars: Fully rounded `9999px` capsules for distinction against structural boxes.

## Components

### 1. Navigation & Sticky Executive Header
- **Top Utility Strip:** Gilded notice bar (`#0F172A` with `#F59E0B` subtle text highlights) detailing legal compliance milestones (e.g., e-invoicing mandate dates) alongside direct phone and support links.
- **Main Bar:** Sticky glassmorphic bar. Left-aligned primary logotype; central links with hover indicator line; right side hosting two distinct anchors:
  - *Espace Client GED:* Outlined navy pill with a subtle security padlock icon.
  - *Devis Gratuit / Contact:* Solid cobalt pill button with micro-hover elevation.

### 2. Pricing & Tier Cards
- **Base Card:** Clean white backdrop, `1rem` corner radius, `1px solid #E2E8F0`. Contains role badge, bold `label-price` Space Grotesk figure, bulleted deliverables with emerald check icons, and a low-profile tertiary button.
- **Featured Card ("Formule Confort"):** Elevated with an ambient blue shadow, primary cobalt top border badge, and a high-contrast solid button. Displays an amber highlight ribbon: "Recommandé".

### 3. Action Buttons & Direct Payment
- **Primary Action:** Solid `#1E40AF` fill, `#FFFFFF` text, `0.5rem` radius, smooth transform on hover (`translateY(-1px)`).
- **Executive Call Button:** Deep navy `#0F172A` fill with contrasting white text and telephone glyph.
- **Prestige / Quote CTA:** Dual gradient or warm amber accent (`#D97706` hover `#B45309`) reserved for commercial acquisition.
- **Direct PayPal Checkout Module:** Dedicated compliant container featuring official PayPal badge and secure transaction micro-copy (`128-bit SSL Encryption`), built into direct subscription checkout flows.

### 4. Input Fields & Lead Generation Form
- Two-column structured responsive form.
- Light slate background (`#F8FAFC`), crisp border (`#CBD5E1`), transitioning to `#1E40AF` with a `0 0 0 3px rgba(30, 64, 175, 0.15)` focus ring.
- Floating or persistent uppercase micro-labels (`label-md`).

### 5. Accordions & Regulatory FAQ
- Seamless white container with divider borders (`#F1F5F9`).
- Left-aligned bold question in `headline-sm`, right-aligned minimal chevron that rotates 180 degrees smoothly on toggle. Expanded panel reveals explanatory copy with subtle indented left border in `#1E40AF`.
